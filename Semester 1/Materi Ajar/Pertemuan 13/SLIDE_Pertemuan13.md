## Filter Konten

| Filter | Cara Aktifkan |
|---|---|
| **SafeSearch** | google.com/preferences |
| **YouTube Restricted** | Settings → Restricted Mode |
| **Instagram Sensitive** | Settings → Sensitive Content → Less |
| **DNS Filtering** | OpenDNS: 208.67.222.123 |

> Filter konten = kendali atas apa yang kamu lihat.
