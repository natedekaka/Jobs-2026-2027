## Materi 2 — Data Cleaning

```
Missing   → ISBLANK() / isi rata-rata
Duplikat  → COUNTIF > 1 → hapus
Outlier   → nilai -10 atau 120 (skala 0-100)
Format    → =PROPER(), =TRIM(), =UPPER()
```

> GIGO: Garbage In, Garbage Out!
