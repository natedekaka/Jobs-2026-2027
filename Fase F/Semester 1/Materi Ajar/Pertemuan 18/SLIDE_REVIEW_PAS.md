# MATERI 2
## Strategi Algoritmik (Pert 8–13)

---

## Greedy

> Pilih solusi **terbaik lokal** di setiap langkah

✅ Penukaran koin, Fractional Knapsack, Activity Selection
❌ Binary Search (bukan greedy)

---

## Divide & Conquer

```
Bagi → Selesaikan → Gabung
```

✅ Merge Sort, Binary Search, Quick Sort
⏱️ Merge Sort: **O(n log n)**

---

## Backtracking

> **Coba semua kemungkinan → mundur jika buntu**

✅ Maze, N-Queens, Permutasi

---

## Graph & BFS/DFS

| Graph | BFS | DFS |
|---|---|---|
| Node + Edge | Queue → level-order | Stack → depth-order |

**Dijkstra**: jarak terpendek dari 1 simpul ke semua simpul
