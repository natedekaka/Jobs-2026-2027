## AVERAGEIF

Rata-rata dengan syarat

```
=AVERAGEIF(range, kriteria, avg_range)
```

---

## COUNTIFS & SUMIFS

Multi-kriteria!

```
=COUNTIFS(range1, krit1, range2, krit2)
=SUMIFS(sum_range, range1, krit1, ...)
```

---

## IF + AND/OR

```excel
=IF(AND(A>=75, B>=80), "Lulus", "Tidak")
=IF(OR(A>=90, B>=85), "A", "B")
```
