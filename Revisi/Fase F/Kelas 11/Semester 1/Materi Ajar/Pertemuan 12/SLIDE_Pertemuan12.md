## Tujuan

---

## VLOOKUP

```
=VLOOKUP(kunci, tabel, kolom, FALSE)
```

Mencocokkan data dari tabel lain

---

## COUNTIF & SUMIF

```
=COUNTIF(range, kriteria)
=SUMIF(range, kriteria, sum_range)
```

| Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|
| COUNTIF | =COUNTIF(E2:E10,">=75") |
| SUMIF | =SUMIF(D2:D10,"L",F2:F10) |

---

## IF Bertingkat

```
=IF(nilai>=85,"A", IF(nilai>=70,"B", IF(nilai>=55,"C","D")))
```
